Embroidery software

StickStudio

From logo to finished stitch file – digitized quickly and cleanly.

  • Satin, fill, running and triple stitch with short stitches on tight curves
  • 28 ready-made embroidery fonts – also on an arc, with satin stitches across the stroke
  • Auto-digitizing from images and logos
  • Fewer trims: hidden travel runs and an optimized stitch order
  • Fabric assistant – stabilizer, needle and stitch density matched to the material
  • Garment templates (T-shirt, polo, hoodie, cap) for customer previews

Formats: DST (Tajima, e.g. Texi), PES (Brother), JEF (Janome), EXP (Melco/Bernina)

CAD + CAM + machine control

FräsStudio

Draw, generate toolpaths, check them in 3D and start cutting – all in one program.

  • Profile, pocket, drilling and surfacing with tabs, ramps and finishing passes
  • V-carving with true depth calculation for razor-sharp lettering
  • 3D reliefs from images and STL models – or generated by AI from ordinary photos
  • 3D material-removal preview with run time and warnings
  • Control your machine directly via USB or Wi-Fi: set zero, probe, change tools
  • Overhead camera – place designs precisely on the real workpiece

For GRBL routers, e.g. Twotrees TTC450 (also with 500 W and 800 W spindles)

Laser software with camera

LaserStudio

Engrave and cut like the big-name programs – only simpler.

  • Layers by color: line, fill, offset fill and image, each with its own settings
  • Photo engraving with 9 dithering modes (Jarvis, Stucki, grayscale …)
  • Camera alignment with height compensation – no re-aligning for thicker material
  • Material test and material library with starting values for your laser
  • Framing, tabs, kerf compensation, text on an arc, serial numbers
  • Preview with run time – see exactly what will be burned before the laser fires

For GRBL lasers, e.g. Mecpow X5 / X5 Pro, diode lasers and CO2 lasers with a GRBL controller

Frequently asked questions

Good to know

Is this a subscription?

No. You buy the program once and can use it permanently. One year of updates is included. After that your version keeps working as normal – new versions are available with an optional update renewal.

What happens after one year?

Nothing bad: your program keeps working just as before. If you want new features and improvements, you renew updates for another year – any time, even later on. We'll remind you by email in good time.

How many computers can I use it on?

One license covers two computers, for example your workshop PC and your laptop. Moving to a new computer? Just deactivate the old one in the program with a single click.

Do I need to be online all the time?

No. Once to activate; after that the program checks the license occasionally in the background. Without internet it runs for up to 30 days. For workshops with no internet at all, we offer offline activation – just get in touch.

Will it work with my machine?

StickStudio exports DST, PES, JEF and EXP – formats almost every embroidery machine understands. FräsStudio and LaserStudio control machines with a GRBL controller, which covers most hobby and small-batch CNC routers and diode lasers. Simply try it free for 14 days with your own machine.

Which languages are the programs available in?

StickStudio, FräsStudio and LaserStudio each come in English, German and Danish (Deutsch, English, Dansk). You can switch the language right inside the program at any time.

Can I buy the programs separately?

Yes – each program is sold and licensed on its own. You only pay for what you actually need.

Can I keep using files from other programs?

Yes: SVG and DXF drawings, images and G-code open directly, and StickStudio also reads existing embroidery files. LaserStudio turns the colors of an SVG file straight into layers.

How do I pay, and will I get an invoice?

You pay securely through our payment provider by credit card, PayPal or Apple Pay. Your invoice showing VAT and your license key arrive instantly by email.
