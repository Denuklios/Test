2. Overview

We only process the data required to operate StickStudio. We do not use any analytics or tracking tools, advertising or marketing cookies, social media plugins or embedded third-party content (e.g. external font, map or video services). All program files, fonts and libraries are served from our own server.

3. Hosting and server log files

The Software and our website are operated on servers of our hosting provider:

{{hosting_provider}}

The hosting provider processes the data exclusively on our behalf and in accordance with our instructions. We have concluded a data processing agreement with it in accordance with Art. 28 GDPR.

Each time our website or the Software is accessed, the web server automatically records the following data in server log files:

  • IP address of the requesting device,
  • date and time of access,
  • requested address (URL) and HTTP method,
  • HTTP status code and amount of data transferred,
  • referrer URL (previously visited page), if transmitted by the browser,
  • browser type and version and operating system (user agent).

The purpose of this processing is to ensure secure and stable operation, in particular to prevent and investigate attacks (e.g. overload or login attempts), and to analyse errors. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest lies in the security and functionality of our service. Log files are automatically deleted after 14 days unless individual entries are needed for longer to investigate a specific security incident; in that case they are deleted as soon as they are no longer required for that purpose. The data are not combined with other data sources for analysis purposes.

Sign-in log, last IP address and country

On registration and sign-in we store in our database (in addition to the server log files) the time, the IP address and the country derived from it (not the location). In the user account only the most recently used IP address with its country and the time of the last activity are stored. The country is determined on our server using a locally stored file; no IP addresses are sent to third parties. Purposes are the detection of abuse (e.g. account sharing, attacks), securing the licence and customer support, as well as statistics in anonymised form (number of customers per country). The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The sign-in log including IP addresses is deleted automatically after 90 days; the last IP address in the user account is deleted with the account.

4. Cookies and local storage in the browser

Session cookie: After you log in, we set a single, technically necessary cookie containing a random session identifier. It is used to recognise you as logged in during your session and to protect your account against unauthorised access. The cookie has the attributes HttpOnly and Secure, is only transmitted over encrypted connections and is not used for tracking. It is deleted when you log out or when the session expires.

Local storage: The Software stores some settings that you make yourself (e.g. language, mouse wheel behaviour, custom hoops, help hints on/off) and, where applicable, a local design library in your browser's storage (localStorage/IndexedDB). These data remain on your device and are not transmitted to us. You can delete them at any time in your browser settings.

Storing and accessing this information on your device is strictly necessary for us to provide the service you have expressly requested; consent is therefore not required (Section 25(2) No. 2 of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act, TDDDG). The legal basis for the associated processing of personal data is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure login).

5. User account

A user account is required to use StickStudio. In this context we process:

  • email address,
  • password – exclusively as a hash value generated with a recognised, salted hashing method; the password itself is not stored and cannot be viewed by us either,
  • date of registration and last login, and start and end of the trial and of the licence,
  • licence status and the payment provider's order reference,
  • language setting and the time at which you accepted the Terms and, where applicable, agreed to the early start of performance (evidence).

The purpose is to set up and manage your account, to provide the Software and to manage the trial and licence. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract or pre-contractual measures). The evidence of consent is also stored on the basis of Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (proof towards authorities and in the event of a dispute).

6. Projects (embroidery designs)

Your projects are stored on our server and assigned to your account (project data in JSON format, including any template images and text you have inserted). To import and export embroidery files (e.g. DST, PES, JEF, EXP), you transfer files to our server, where they are processed only for conversion and are not stored permanently unless you save them as a project.

Projects usually do not contain personal data; however, this may be the case if, for example, you enter names for name embroidery. We do not look at your projects unless this is necessary to fix an error you have reported and you have agreed, or we are legally obliged to do so. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

7. Payment processing

Paid licences are processed via {{payment_provider}}. When you order, you are redirected to a payment page of {{payment_provider}} or this page is displayed as an overlay. The data entered there (e.g. name, billing address, email address, payment details such as credit card or bank details, VAT ID where applicable) are collected and processed directly by {{payment_provider}}. We do not receive complete payment details.

From {{payment_provider}} we only receive the information required to perform the contract: email address, name and country where applicable, order number, product purchased, amount, payment and subscription status, and term.

If {{payment_provider}} acts as reseller and seller towards you ("merchant of record", e.g. Lemon Squeezy), {{payment_provider}} is an independent controller for the processing involved in the purchase, payment, invoicing, tax remittance and fraud prevention; its privacy policy applies in this respect. If we sell directly using a payment service provider (e.g. Stripe), that provider processes payment data partly as an independent controller (e.g. to comply with financial regulations and to prevent fraud) and partly on our behalf; details can be found in the payment provider's privacy notice.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract) and Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR (tax and commercial law obligations). Where {{payment_provider}} transfers data to countries outside the EU/EEA (e.g. the USA), this is done on the basis of an adequacy decision of the European Commission (e.g. the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, if the provider is certified) or appropriate safeguards, in particular EU standard contractual clauses (Art. 45, 46 GDPR).

8. Sending emails

We only send emails where they are necessary for your account and your contract, in particular:

  • emails for resetting your password (at your request, with a link valid for a limited time),
  • order and contract confirmations, confirmations of cancellations and withdrawals,
  • notices of the upcoming end of the trial or licence, renewals, changes to prices or Terms, and important security or maintenance information.

We do not send newsletters or advertising emails. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; legally required notices are sent on the basis of Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR. Emails are sent via the mail server (SMTP) of our hosting or email provider ({{hosting_provider}}), which acts for us as a processor in accordance with Art. 28 GDPR.

9. Contacting us

If you contact us by email, we process your email address, your name (if provided) and the content of your message in order to answer your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your enquiry relates to a contract, and otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering enquiries). We delete the correspondence as soon as it is no longer required, at the latest after the statutory retention periods have expired (see section 10).

10. Storage periods

We only store personal data for as long as necessary for the respective purpose or as required by statutory retention obligations:

  • Server log files: 14 days (exception: investigation of a specific security incident, see section 3).
  • Session cookie: until you log out or the session expires.
  • Account data and projects: until your account is deleted. After the trial or licence has expired, the account and projects are retained in view-only mode. We delete accounts that have not been used for more than 24 months thereafter, after prior notice by email. Deleted data are removed from backups after 30 days at the latest.
  • Password reset links: expire after a short time (e.g. one hour) and are then deleted.
  • Invoices and accounting records (where we are the seller ourselves): 8 years; commercial and business correspondence (e.g. order confirmations, correspondence relating to the contract): 6 years, in each case from the end of the calendar year (Section 147 of the German Fiscal Code, Section 257 of the German Commercial Code, Section 14b of the German VAT Act). During this time, the data are blocked for other purposes.
  • Evidence of consent (e.g. to the early start of performance) and of cancellations: until the limitation periods for possible claims have expired (generally 3 years from the end of the calendar year in which the contract ended).

11. Recipients and transfers to third countries

Your data are only transferred to the recipients named in this policy: our hosting and email provider (processors), the payment provider or reseller {{payment_provider}} and – where necessary – our tax advisers (to fulfil legal obligations) and authorities where we are legally obliged to do so. We do not sell your data or pass it on for advertising purposes.

Our servers are located within the European Union or the European Economic Area. Data are only transferred to third countries in the context of payment processing (section 7) and only subject to the conditions of Art. 44 et seq. GDPR.

12. Obligation to provide data

Providing an email address and a password is necessary to conclude and perform the contract. Without these data we cannot provide you with a user account. To purchase a licence, the data requested by the payment provider are also required.

13. No automated decision-making

There is no automated decision-making, including profiling, within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR. The automatic switch to view-only mode after the trial or licence has expired is merely the contractually agreed consequence of the expiry of time.

14. Data security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, in particular:

  • encrypted transmission of all data via TLS (HTTPS),
  • storage of passwords only as salted hash values using a method suitable for passwords,
  • restriction of access to servers and data to the necessary minimum,
  • protection of the session cookie by the attributes HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite,
  • limiting login attempts, and regular security updates and backups.

15. Your rights

You have the following rights with regard to your personal data:

  • right of access (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR) – you can also download your projects yourself in JSON format at any time,
  • right to object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR, see below),
  • right to withdraw consent with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR), where processing is based on consent.

To exercise your rights, an informal message to {{email}} is sufficient.

Right to object: Where we process your data on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you have the right to object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will then no longer process the data unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds for the processing which override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing serves the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.
