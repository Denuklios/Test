This is an English translation of our German withdrawal policy (Widerrufsbelehrung).

Consumers (Section 13 of the German Civil Code, BGB) have a right of withdrawal when concluding a paid licence agreement for StickStudio, as set out below. Businesses have no right of withdrawal.

Right of withdrawal

You have the right to withdraw from this contract within fourteen days without giving any reason.

The withdrawal period is fourteen days from the day the contract is concluded.

To exercise your right of withdrawal, you must inform us ({{company}}, {{owner}}, {{street}}, {{zip_city}}, {{country}}, email: {{email}}, phone: {{phone}}) of your decision to withdraw from this contract by means of a clear statement (e.g. a letter sent by post or an email). You may use the attached model withdrawal form, but this is not mandatory. You can also declare your withdrawal using the "Withdraw from contract" button in your user account; in that case we will confirm receipt of your withdrawal by email without undue delay.

To meet the withdrawal deadline, it is sufficient for you to send your communication concerning your exercise of the right of withdrawal before the withdrawal period has expired.

Effects of withdrawal

If you withdraw from this contract, we shall reimburse to you all payments received from you without undue delay and in any event not later than fourteen days from the day on which we are informed about your decision to withdraw from this contract. We will carry out such reimbursement using the same means of payment as you used for the initial transaction, unless you have expressly agreed otherwise; in any event, you will not incur any fees as a result of such reimbursement.

If you requested that the services begin during the withdrawal period, you shall pay us an amount which is in proportion to what has been provided until you have communicated to us your withdrawal from this contract, in comparison with the full coverage of the contract.

On withdrawal, the licence is deactivated; your user account and projects are retained in view-only mode (see Terms and Conditions).

Early expiry of the right of withdrawal

For contracts for digital services (provision of the StickStudio software as an online service), the right of withdrawal expires when we have fully performed the service and only began performance after you gave your express consent and at the same time acknowledged that you will lose your right of withdrawal once we have fully performed the contract (Section 356(4) BGB).

For contracts for the supply of digital content not supplied on a tangible medium, the right of withdrawal expires once we have begun performance of the contract after you have expressly consented to us beginning performance before the end of the withdrawal period, acknowledged that you thereby lose your right of withdrawal once performance begins, and we have provided you with a confirmation of the contract on a durable medium (e.g. by email) (Section 356(5) BGB).

Consent during checkout

So that your licence can be activated immediately after ordering, during checkout and before you click "Order with obligation to pay" we ask you for the following declaration (checkbox, not pre-ticked; you can also order without ticking it – activation then only takes place after the withdrawal period has expired):

☐ I expressly request and agree that {{company}} begins performance of the contract before the end of the withdrawal period and activates my licence immediately. I acknowledge that if I withdraw, I must pay a reasonable amount for the period of use provided up to that point, and that my right of withdrawal expires once the contract has been fully performed. To the extent that the contract concerns the supply of digital content, I acknowledge that by giving this consent I lose my right of withdrawal once performance begins.

The order confirmation we send you by email contains the content of the contract and a confirmation of this consent and acknowledgement (Section 312f(3) BGB).

If your order is processed by a reseller acting as the seller towards you ("merchant of record", currently {{payment_provider}}), you can also declare your withdrawal to that reseller; in that case the refund is made via {{payment_provider}}.
